Arc Tunes 2 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Developer: Justin Novikow · Bundle ID com.jnovikow.ArcTunes2

Short version: Arc Tunes 2 does not require an account. Game progress stays on your device. The free game shows Google AdMob ads. Ad requests are non-personalized. Arc Tunes 2 does not sell your information.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves gameplay on the device, including:

  • Scores and endless bests
  • Campaign progress and stars
  • Diamonds, free lives, skins, and trophies earned in the game
  • The mute setting, and whether you have seen the how-to

That save stays on the device unless you delete the app or restore a device backup that includes it. Justin Novikow does not receive a copy of this save.

2. Advertising

Arc Tunes 2 shows ads from Google AdMob. A rewarded video can continue a run. A full-screen ad can play after a final game over. The app asks Google for non-personalized ads. Google may still process an advertising identifier and technical data about the device, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy. Justin Novikow does not receive your name or email from these ads.

3. What this version does not do

  • No Arc Tunes 2 account, email, or password
  • No tracking prompt that follows you across other companies’ apps for personalized ads
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No sale of personal information

The game’s pictures and music are inside the app. It does not load a website to play.

4. Children

Arc Tunes 2 is a general-audience timing game. It is not directed at children under 13 as a service that collects personal information. It does not ask for a name, email, or other contact details.

5. Changes

If a later version adds accounts or other collection, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
