How to play

  • A ring closes on the dot as it reaches the glowing gate. Tap when it closes.
  • EARLY and LATE tell you which way you missed.
  • Tracks arch over themselves. Some bounce, rebuild, rotate, or reverse.
  • Orbs sit on the path for a few seconds. Gold ones help. Red ones hurt.
  • Leap warps the dot to where you tap on the track. Arm it with the gold leap control.
  • Campaigns are five routes: Overpass, Abyss, Rise of the Robots, Switchyard, and Glassworks.
  • Endless mode keeps going. You have three lives.

Common questions

Progress disappeared?
Scores, diamonds, free lives, and route progress are stored on this device. Deleting the app removes that save. An iCloud or device backup may restore it if the app was included in the backup.

Account
There is no Arc Tunes 2 account. The leaderboard on this version is the scores saved on your device.

Ads
The free game can show a short Google AdMob video. You can watch one to continue a run. A full-screen ad can also play after a final game over. You can still continue with a free life or diamonds. Requests are non-personalized. See the Privacy Policy.

Sound
Use the speaker button on the home screen, or the M key, to mute.

Something broken?
Email jnovikow@gmail.com with your device, iOS version, and what you were playing. A screenshot helps.
